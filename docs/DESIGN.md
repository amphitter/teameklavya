## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an energetic, aspirational, and forward-moving aesthetic tailored for modern student, creator, and builder communities. It balances structural clarity with youthful dynamism, pairing crisp typography with luminous chromatic gradients and softly sculpted surfaces.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Students, hackathon builders, tech organizers, creative professionals, and networking attendees seeking growth, inspiration, and belonging.
- **Atmosphere:** Optimistic, social, reliable, and premium. The interface communicates frictionless progression—transforming complex event discovery and onboarding flows into intuitive, inviting interactions.

### Movement & Visual Identity
The system combines **Corporate/Modern structure** with **Vibrant Layered Glass/Neomorphic undertones**:
- Crisp, hyper-readable typography ground the functional utility of event management and profile building.
- Multi-stop radiant gradients (Royal Blue to Deep Purple to Electric Magenta) serve as active focal points across primary actions, active indicators, and hero brand moments.
- Generous corner radii, floating cards with ambient deep-indigo shadows, and crisp surface separations create an inviting, app-like visual rhythm across both web and native mobile touchpoints.

## Colors

The color palette is built around high-saturation chromatic transitions supported by structural deep navies and crisp clean surfaces.

### Primary Palette
- **Primary Navy (`#0B1235`):** The grounding anchor used for dominant display headings, top navigation brand marks, and high-emphasis body text.
- **Deep Navy (`#111936`):** Secondary structural dark, used for layered dark surfaces, modals, and high-contrast card headers.
- **Primary Blue (`#2563FF`):** The driving identity color, signifying trust, connection, and primary interactivity.
- **Electric Blue (`#1677FF`):** Interactive hover states, links, and focused input indicators.

### Accent & Gradient Spectrum
- **Purple (`#6C35FF`):** Transition anchor that bridges foundational blue into creative energy.
- **Violet (`#7C3AED`):** Secondary badges, category chips, and selected state backdrops.
- **Pink / Magenta (`#D946EF`):** Energy highlight for badges, active tabs, sparkles, and graduation points in multi-stop linear fills.
- **Primary Brand Gradient:** Linear gradient set at `135deg`, smoothly running from `#2563FF` (0%) to `#6C35FF` (52%) to `#D946EF` (100%). Used exclusively for key CTAs, hero badges, active navigation indicators, and celebratory highlights.

### Functional & Surface Tones
- **Canvas Background (`#F8FAFF`):** Light, slightly tinted cool-blue neutral providing softness compared to harsh clinical white.
- **Surface Pure (`#FFFFFF`):** High-level elevation cards, floating sheets, inputs, and popovers.
- **Lavender Tint (`#EDE9FE`):** Soft focus fills, selected tag states, and secondary avatar frames.
- **Light Blue Tint (`#EFF6FF`):** Unread states, message bubbles, and informational chips.
- **Border Surface (`#E4E9F4`):** Soft, structural separator ensuring crisp contrast between cards, inputs, and canvas.
- **Muted Text (`#64709A`):** Low-strain neutral for secondary labels, placeholders, timestamps, and breadcrumbs.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on **Inter** across all display, body, and functional interface tiers. It pairs tight negative letter-spacing for large titles with crisp, highly legible micro-details for cards, tags, and onboarding flows.

### Typographic Rhythm & Scaling
- **Display & Hero Headings:** Employs heavy font weights (700 and 800) with tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) for high impact. In marketing and onboarding heroes, keywords can feature gradient text fills running horizontally from `#2563FF` to `#6C35FF`.
- **Form & Input Typography:** Form inputs utilize `15px` to `16px` font sizing to prevent unwanted mobile zoom behaviors, paired with a reliable `24px` line-height for optimal vertical alignment with leading icons.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Category chips, status badges, and timestamp elements use font weights of `600` combined with slight positive letter spacing (`0.01em` to `0.03em`) to maintain sharp legibility at reduced scale.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout structure is engineered around an 8-point base rhythm, catering to dense data views (event calendars, attendee lists) as well as spacious, guided flows (authentication modals, onboarding wizards).

### Layout Philosophy & Adaptation
- **Desktop (12-Column Responsive Grid):** Uses a max content container of `1280px` centered with variable margins (minimum `24px` / `1.5rem`) and `24px` gutters. Multi-column auth layouts divide 50/50 between illustrative visual storytelling on the left and form containers on the right.
- **Tablet (8-Column Fluid Grid):** Collapses dual-column split flows into stacked containers. Gutter widths adjust to `16px` (`1rem`) and lateral screen margins preserve `24px` safe bounds.
- **Mobile (4-Column Layout):** Form factors under `640px` use edge-to-edge flow containers with `16px` margins. Action buttons and input fields adapt to full-width block behaviors (`w-full`) to optimize thumb-zone reachability. Sticky bottom navigation and progress bars lock directly to safe viewport bounds.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses layered ambient shadows with cool indigo undertones rather than generic neutral grays, achieving a modern, luminous presence.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Level (0px / Flat):** Background tone `#F8FAFF` hosts passive structural surfaces and subtle watermark patterns (such as dotted arrays and faint gradient blobs).
2. **Interactive Cards & Modals (Elevated Low):**
   - **Shadow:** `0px 4px 16px rgba(24, 39, 75, 0.04), 0px 1px 2px rgba(24, 39, 75, 0.02)`
   - **Border:** `1px solid #E4E9F4`
   - **Background:** `#FFFFFF`
   - Used for quiet event feed cards, inactive chips, and input wrappers.
3. **Hero Auth & Key Floating Containers (Elevated Medium):**
   - **Shadow:** `0px 10px 40px rgba(24, 39, 75, 0.08), 0px 2px 6px rgba(24, 39, 75, 0.03)`
   - **Border:** `1px solid #E4E9F4`
   - **Background:** `#FFFFFF`
   - Applied to prominent login cards, profile creation frames, and floating dropdowns.
4. **Active Badges & CTA Floats (Elevated High / Glow):**
   - **Shadow:** `0px 8px 24px rgba(37, 99, 255, 0.25)`
   - Used under gradient buttons and active multi-select indicators to signal direct interactivity.
5. **Backdrop Overlays & Dimming:**
   - Overlay fills leverage `#0B1235` at `40%` opacity blended with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` to preserve lightness and focus attention on centered modals.

## Shapes

The design system relies on a welcoming, modern curved architecture with deliberate visual hierarchy across scales:

- **Large Structural Containers & Cards:** Configured with `18px` to `24px` border radii. Larger radiused bounds give event cards, hero image cutouts, and modal wrappers an inviting, touch-friendly appearance.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons & Inputs):** Standardized strictly to `10px` – `12px` border radii. This preserves crisp form geometry while complementing the softer outer cards.
- **Pills & Status Chips:** Fully rounded (`9999px`) for category filter tags, interest pills, user count badges, and verification status chips.
- **Image Frames & Hero Collage Cuts:** Rotated photo frames within hero collages utilize `20px` corner rounding paired with thick white border strokes (`4px` to `6px`) and diffused ambient shadows.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Gradient CTA:**
  - Background: `linear-gradient(135deg, #2563FF 0%, #6C35FF 52%, #D946EF 100%)`.
  - Content: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), `label-lg` font weight, centered with a trailing icon arrow.
  - Sizing: `48px` height on mobile, `48px` to `52px` on desktop; `12px` border radius; zero border.
  - Hover: Subtle brightness lift (`1.05`) with expanded glow shadow `0px 10px 24px rgba(108, 53, 255, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Outlined:**
  - Background: `#FFFFFF` or `transparent`.
  - Border: `1px solid #E4E9F4`. Text: `#0B1235`.
  - Hover: Border color transitions to `#2563FF` with light blue tint fill (`#EFF6FF`).
- **Social Auth Buttons:**
  - White surface background with `1px solid #E4E9F4`, `12px` radius, `48px` height. Houses native provider SVG icon centered or left-aligned with neutral navy label.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Container Sizing:** `48px` height, `10px` to `12px` border radius, `#FFFFFF` background.
- **Border States:**
  - Resting: `1px solid #E4E9F4`.
  - Focused: `1.5px solid #2563FF` accompanied by a focus ring glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 255, 0.12)`.
  - Error: `1.5px solid #EF4444` with error ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`.
- **Icons & Affordances:** Leading icons rendered in `#64709A` at `20px`. Trailing action triggers (e.g., eye password toggles) are interactive with hover feedback.

### Chips & Interest Tags
- **Selectable Interest Chips (e.g., Hackathons, AI/ML, Design):**
  - Resting: Light border `#E4E9F4`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0B1235`, pill-shaped (`9999px`), `36px` height.
  - Selected: Filled background `#2563FF` or `#6C35FF` with crisp white text, or soft lavender tint `#EDE9FE` with deep violet text `#7C3AED` and matching border.

### Cards & Feed Items
- **Event & Community Cards:**
  - Outer shell: `18px` to `24px` radius, pure white background, ambient shadow `0 10px 40px rgba(24, 39, 75, 0.08)`.
  - Header: Rich media banner clipped to top border radius.
  - Meta Info: Micro avatar stacks, date badges, and attendee count pills clustered with `space-sm` gaps.

### Step Indicators & Onboarding Progress
- **Timeline Progression:** Multi-step wizard dots linked via `2px` connecting tracks.
- Active steps display deep blue `#2563FF` or vibrant gradient fills with white step numbers. Completed steps display checkmark icons with green/blue tint accents. Upcoming steps use muted `#E4E9F4` circles with `#64709A` numerals.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Geometry:** `20px` by `20px` with `6px` radius for checkboxes; full circular for radios.
- **States:** Checked states fill with `#2563FF` displaying a centered white tick mark. Focus states introduce soft blue expansion halo.
